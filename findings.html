LAUNDRO

A comprehensive report on the development and evaluation of Laundro.

Results, Discussion, and Recommendation

This section outlines the consolidated findings extracted from our Heuristic Evaluation using Jakob Nielsen's 10 Usability Heuristics, alongside the analyzed metrics from the user survey testing.

Survey Analysis & SUS Score

The app received a Final System Usability Scale (SUS) Score of 73.63/100. A score above 68 indicates that the application has good usability, is easy to learn, and users felt confident navigating the system.

Major Survey Themes

  • Ease of Use & Clean UI: Users repeatedly praised the clean layout and straightforward booking process, emphasizing that the prototype successfully communicated its purpose without confusing users.
  • Simplicity and Convenience: Recognized as the app's biggest strength. The app was highly valued for its practicality and potential to reduce effort and time, especially for busy college students.
  • Beginner-Friendly: Even respondents who rarely or never use laundry services found the interface understandable and easy to navigate.

Summary of Heuristic Results

Based on the evaluation, the Laundro App received a total of 47 heuristic findings. The results were overwhelmingly positive, with 46 findings marked as "Passes" and 41 items rated as "Severity 0 (No Problem)". The app excels in maintaining a match between the system and real-world laundry concepts, and providing clear visibility of system status. However, 6 cosmetic issues (Severity 1) were identified that require minor design refinements to optimize the user experience.

Key Issues Identified & Discussion

  • Consistency and Standards (Heuristic #4): While mostly consistent, some screens (e.g., home dashboard vs. booking form) show slight variations in button styles, padding, and alignment.
  • Error Prevention (Heuristic #5): Forms such as pickup address and contact number lack inline validation rules. There is also no explicit confirmation dialog for critical actions like final booking submission.
  • Recognition Rather Than Recall (Heuristic #6): During the booking flow, users must remember previously selected details (e.g., chosen service type) because there is limited step-by-step guidance or progress indication.
  • Quantity Selection Improvement (Survey Insight): A user noted the need to type item quantities directly instead of repeatedly pressing "+" or "-" buttons for faster selection.
  • Payment Method Clarification (Survey Insight): The payment process requires better onboarding and clearer instructions due to confusion reported during presentation testing.

Recommendations & Design Improvements

Identified Issue Suggested Improvement
Inconsistent Button Styles & Padding Standardize the CSS for action buttons and ensure uniform margin/padding across all dashboard and form screens.
Lack of Form Validation & Confirmation Add inline error messages (e.g., highlighting invalid phone numbers in red) and implement a "Review Order" modal before final submission.
Slow Quantity Input Allow manual keyboard entry for item quantities instead of strictly relying on increment buttons.
Unclear Payment Options Improve payment instructions and enhance the interface visibility for payment options during onboarding.
Missing Help Features Add a subtle "Help / Tooltip" icon next to complex tasks, and create a basic FAQ page accessible from the main menu or profile tab.
